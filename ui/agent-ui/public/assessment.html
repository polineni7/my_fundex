MYFUNDEX · TRADER ASSESSMENTS

Assess your equity trading skills

MyFundex uses a staged simulated trading assessment to evaluate applicants for potential contracted trading work with the firm.

How the assessment works

  1. Review the available assessment plans, fees and objectives.
  2. Enrol and pay the assessment fee through the configured payment provider.
  3. Complete each simulated trading stage within the stated risk limits.
  4. After passing, proceed to the firm's contract and eligibility review.

Passing does not guarantee employment, a contract, income or live trading access. Any live allocation requires separate approval and an authorized broker arrangement.

What the assessment fee means

The displayed fee is for the selected assessment. Simulated assessment capital is not a cash deposit, investment balance or withdrawable earning. Review the applicable terms, cancellation/refund policy and fee details before paying. A fresh assessment after failure may require another fee.

Two-step and three-step plans

Each plan defines its stage targets, daily and total loss limits, minimum trading days and any time limit. The available plan version contains the applicable figures; this page does not promise a fixed fee or return.

Earnings after contract approval

Where contracted trading is approved, the agreement and plan terms specify the trader share, firm share and applicable deductions. Per-trade estimates are provisional. Only verified settlements, after charges and losses, become eligible for withdrawal.
